I sent a careful message through the night,
Where Agent System held its measured course;
The OpenAI channel showed no light,
And silence mocked each neatly routed source.

I traced each token, header, hook, and call,
While hopeful logs declared the message sent;
Yet nothing crossed the darkened channel wall,
As though the wire denied what “success” meant.

Again I patched the path from end to end,
Again the green checks promised all was well;
Each clean report became a faithless friend,
A polished little liar in the shell.

Then one small ping arrived, both sharp and clear;
The channel spoke—and dawn was almost here.
